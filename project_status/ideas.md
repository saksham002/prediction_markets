## Strategy
- **Cross-leg latency arb at goals (event E)** [REVIEW]: when a goal moves one leg of a 3-outcome WC event, the other legs sit at STALE quotes for a short window before they reprice consistently. Aggress the stale legs in the correct direction, then liquidate passively. Evidence (scratch/leg_arb.py, 8 WC games): leader→follower lag **median 55 ms**, stale-quote edge **median ~12¢** (max 88¢), resting size median 230 (up to 8.8k) contracts, stale window **median ~58 ms** (some 300–550 ms); ~half the follower-cases are realistically capturable (window ≥50 ms, real size). Near risk-free IF order RTT < window. Spec: **aggressive entry on detection of E + immediate passive liquidation WITHOUT alpha; no trading except on E or liquidation** — so no directional hold (avoids the tail risk below). Open design Qs under discussion: (1) E-detection logic (≥5¢ best-price move in 1s + ≥75% of intervening levels cleared by trade/cancel in the move direction, fired mid-sweep); (2) order RTT to Kalshi = the make/break number; (3) follower sweep depth = take levels until price reaches estimated cross-leg-fair minus fee/safety margin, capped by liquidation capacity; (4) vs passive: needs latency-aware backtest. Distinct, repeatable edge vs the passive strategy's directional luck (see below).
- **Passive obi strategy is ~88% directional, ~12% spread** (scratch/pnl_decomp.py, obi best @ $1000): gross PnL decomposes IN spread +460 / directional +2761 (14%/86%), OUT spread +390 / directional +3101 (11%/89%). Spread (MM edge) is small but ALWAYS +ve (+$45–235/game); directional (accumulated position × mid moves) is large but VOLATILE (−161 to +2318) — wins KORCZE/QATSUI/AUSTUR (correct read), loses MEXRSA/CANBIH (wrong read). So the "+OOS" is mostly directional luck on 2–3 games, exposed to sport-surprise tail risk; only ~$100/game is reliable MM edge. Motivates the arb (genuine edge) and skepticism of the passive OOS number. [REVIEW]
- **3-leg soccer lock netting** [REVIEW]: buy A-win + B-win + Draw below $1 combined = riskless lock; extend pair-risk netting to triples. Cuts capital usage on WC games and turns the budget guard less binding. Moderate code change in SingleMM → "EventMM".
- **Per-league parameters**: once ≥1 week of WC + NHL/NBA data exists, fit thresholds/sizes per league (allowed: per-league, not per-game). Soccer books behave differently (draw leg is mean-reverty).
- **Pregame vs in-game regime split**: same config, two parameter sets switched at game start. Pregame books are tight/slow; in-game vol dominates marks. Needs the regime tag in state.csv (have game_start estimates).
- **Combo gates on lasso weights**: lasso output × sign-agreement gate (combo_om/combo_obi already implemented) — test once lasso weights exist.
- **Queue-aware sizing**: scale size by expected queue wait (displayed ÷ recent trade rate) instead of alpha magnitude.
- **Causal normalization of flow alphas (agg, tfma)** — TESTED (wc_sweep_88, Jun 16): implemented net/gross flow ratio (signed/unsigned EMA ∈ [−1,1]) as `agg_ratio`/`tfma_pw_ratio`. **It FIXED the cross-game transfer** (raw tfma +3998 IN/−4014 OUT → tfma_ratio +308/+238 consistent; agg_ratio +2137/+2148) — the scale-free ratio made in-sample thresholds transfer. **But once honest, flow has NO usable edge**: realized-net negative (agg_ratio −243, tfma_ratio −1212 OUT) and both lose badly to the no-skew `blind` baseline. So normalization was diagnostic — it removed the mark-to-market illusion and confirmed agg/tfma flow isn't a profitable MM signal. Rejected as a skew alpha; the normalization machinery (gross EMAs, `_ratio` names) stays for reuse.
- **obi_dev = obi − obi_ma (deviation) is the leading alpha** [REVIEW] (wc_sweep_88): obi_dev_60s is the ONLY alpha beating the no-skew blind baseline OOS (marginal realized-net +434, net +516); overall best-in (+5267 rn IN / +3164 OUT), beats raw obi (which trails blind OOS). 60s is the sweet-spot HL (> 5s, > 300s). CAVEAT: blind itself is +2730 rn OOS at $1000 (common bounded-directional/spread component dominates), so obi_dev_60s's marginal +434 over 8 games is real but modest — needs a held-out forward-test + directional/spread decomp before trusting.
- **agg_dev gate (obi_dev AND agg_dev) — TESTED & REJECTED** (Jun 21, `wc_sweep_aggdev216`, 21-6, $250, free_budget=true): added `agg_dev_<hl> = agg_<hl> − EMA_<hl>(agg_<hl>)` as a SECOND symmetric block-gate alongside obi_dev (a side is quoted only if BOTH clear their pctile threshold). Result: agg_dev does NOT help OOS — top-5 OUT configs are ALL obi_dev-only; for matched obi_hl the AND-gate **consistently lowers** OUT PnL; the best obi+agg config (`B_obi60s_agg60s_p75` +187.6 OUT) is an OOS-luck outlier (IN −16.3). agg_dev is active (changes fills/PnL) but mostly blocks profitable obi entries → no added edge. Overall best = `obi_dev_60s` thr 0.363514 (obi-only). Like the flow-alpha rejection above, the agg machinery stays for reuse. See experiments.md Jun 21.

## Rejected (evidence)
- Aggressive/taker strategies (gross-negative everywhere). Depth quoting (0 fills on majors). Ladder splits. Improve-a-tick on entries. OBI smoothing. Raw (unweighted) TFMA. Per-game parameter fitting (overfits; also disallowed).
- **Skewing on agg or tfma flow** (wc_sweep_66, Jun 16, 6/6 split, post unsupported-level + EPS fixes): both are mark-to-market illusions — best-in net is +IN but **realized-net is negative even in-sample** (agg −2539, tfma −1703) and OOS blows up (tfma −5730 rn). The +IN net is open directional inventory marked favorably at FT, reverses OOS. Only **obi** has +realized-net both in and out (OOS rn +1674).
- **Gating obi on agg sign (agree_agg_1s, 1s HL)**: ~breakeven OOS (rn +129) ≪ raw obi (+1674) — agg-confirmation discards good obi signal (same pattern as obi×momentum gating). Blind/symmetric quoting also loses (no skew → no edge). → obi-only stands.
